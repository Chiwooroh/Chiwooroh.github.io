Research

My research examines how observed driving and travel behavior can be represented, generated, and evaluated in transportation models. Across behavior analysis and mobility synthesis, automated vehicle evaluation, and traffic safety, I use vehicle trajectories, travel surveys, location-based mobility data, sensor observations, and microscopic traffic simulation to develop and validate behaviorally plausible models. A complete list of publications is available on the Publications page.

LLM-Based Behavior Analysis & Mobility Synthesis

Analyzing driving and travel behavior and synthesizing individual-level mobility patterns from vehicle trajectories, travel surveys, and location-based data using large language models. Trajectory and survey records document observed movement but do not directly identify the latent behavioral states or the contextual factors behind it. I use clustering and LLM-based conditional generation to characterize heterogeneous driving patterns and to construct individual-level travel chains, and evaluate the results by comparing generated and observed distributions at the trajectory level and at the aggregate travel level.

Automated Vehicle Simulation & Evaluation

Evaluating automated vehicle behavior, regulatory compliance, and interactions with human-driven vehicles through microscopic traffic simulation. This work implements driving logic that reflects traffic laws and regulations, measures compliance using indicators such as speed limit adherence, and examines merging and cooperative behavior between automated and human-driven vehicles. Simulation-based evaluation allows scenarios that are difficult to observe on the road to be examined under controlled conditions.

Traffic Safety & Operations

Assessing crash risk, road-user interactions, traffic conditions, and operational performance using crash, trajectory, sensor, and simulation data. Crash records provide observed safety outcomes but are infrequent and lagging, while microscopic traffic simulation supports the analysis of conflicts and operational conditions that may not be sufficiently represented in historical data. Related research addresses pedestrian trajectory prediction, crash severity modeling from structured and unstructured records, and lane-level traffic-state estimation using vehicle-mounted LiDAR.
