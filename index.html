Chiwoo Roh

Transportation Researcher | Behavior Modeling, Automated Vehicles, and Traffic Safety

Republic of Korea

My research focuses on LLM-based behavior analysis and mobility synthesis, automated vehicle simulation and evaluation, and traffic safety and operations. I use vehicle trajectories, crash records, travel surveys, location-based mobility data, and microscopic traffic simulation to study driving and travel behavior, assess crash risk and road-user interactions, and analyze traffic conditions and congestion.

I received my M.S. in Transportation Engineering from Ajou University, where I conducted research in Movelab under the supervision of Prof. Jaehyun So. I previously worked as a Researcher in the Transport Big Data Department at the Korea Transport Institute, focusing on human mobility prediction, privacy-preserving mobility data synthesis, and congestion estimation using large-scale mobility data.

Research interests

LLM-Based Behavior Analysis & Mobility Synthesis

Analyzing driving and travel behavior and synthesizing individual-level mobility patterns from vehicle trajectories, travel surveys, and location-based data using large language models.

Automated Vehicle Simulation & Evaluation

Evaluating automated vehicle behavior, regulatory compliance, and interactions with human-driven vehicles through microscopic traffic simulation.

Traffic Safety & Operations

Assessing crash risk, road-user interactions, traffic conditions, and operational performance using crash, trajectory, sensor, and simulation data.
